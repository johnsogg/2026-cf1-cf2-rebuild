# Nuance (Player's Choice)

This week we are giving you a chance to do some exploration. In class, we're
covering images and sound, including how to upload them to the p5 editor, load
the assets, and then use them.

Please pick an idea that intrigues you, or some aspect of coding that has given
you trouble, and try to make something interesting.

I suggest you check out [OpenProcessing.org](https://openprocessing.org/) and
the [examples on p5js.org](https://p5js.org/examples/) for ideas on what others
are working on. There are a lot of people who are really showing off on those
sites (and that's good!) but don't be intimidated if you feel like you're not
there yet.

## Rubric

The only rubric here is that you have to turn in something that you can defend
as having given you a learning experience. This isn't something we can grade by
remote control, so consider this one to be pass/fail - except me and your LA
will see it, and if you turn in "hello world", we will be displeased.

I do suggest you try applying your artistic interests on this one. Make images
elsewhere (photographs or Photoshop, Illustrator, etc) or try your hand at using
sounds. Maybe both? Think of this as a no-risk practice round for the first
project that is coming up.

## Turn it in on Canvas

As always, provide the editor link to your p5 sketch into the homework box in
Canvas.
