Computers follow instructions precisely and literally. They do exactly what you tell them — no more, no less. This makes them incredibly powerful and incredibly frustrating in equal measure.

The sketch below is a simple example: the computer draws an orange circle at wherever the cursor happens to be. Every time `draw()` runs, it checks `mouseX` and `mouseY`, and puts the circle there. That's it. No guessing, no interpretation — just following instructions.
