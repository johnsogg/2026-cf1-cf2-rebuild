# Language

I'm not an expert on this topic, but I'd bet that spoken language was the
original disruptive technology. We can make funny noises through our mouths and
other people not only _hear_ it but also _understand_ it. And then you can have
a _dialog_ to talk about food, shelter, and gossip. That first 'language'
startup must have absolutely crushed it in the Neolithic.

For language to work, you need an agreed upon system. Words represent ideas:
nouns for things and concepts, verbs for actions, then you have adjectives,
adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, all that. But we need more than just a
dictionary of words and their meanings. We need structure.

Words can't come in an arbitrary order. Don't believe me?

> Arbitrary order an words come can't in. Me believe don't?

Grammar gives rules for how to string together words.

Written language came later.
